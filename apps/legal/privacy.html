Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Writely, we value your privacy. We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and college details provided during registration.
  • Transaction Data: Details of assignments, bids, and financial transactions (payouts and refunds).
  • Usage Data: Information on how you interact with our platform, including AI support chat history.
  • Location Data: City and pincode provided to match writers with local assignments.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • To facilitate the academic marketplace between Seekers and Writers.
  • To process payments securely via our partner, Cashfree.
  • To provide AI-powered assistance and dispute resolution.
  • To ensure the safety and security of our community by preventing fraud.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion at any time via your profile settings. Upon request:

  • Your personal profile will be anonymized.
  • Financial records will be retained as required by law for audit purposes.
  • Your active sessions will be immediately revoked.

4. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including Firebase-backed authentication and server-side validation, to protect your data from unauthorized access.
